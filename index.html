About Fly Bayou

Fly Bayou began as a local Drosophila research meeting connecting laboratories in Louisiana. Since its founding in 2020, the community has expanded to more than 30 laboratories across multiple states. Monthly virtual meetings provide opportunities to share discoveries, discuss new techniques, and foster scientific collaboration.
