About Fly Bayou

From a small, local joint fly meeting that started in the New Orleans and Baton Rouge area in 2020, Fly Bayou has grown into a regional network of Drosophila researchers spanning multiple states. Monthly virtual meetings provide opportunities to present research, exchange ideas, build collaborations, and support trainees throughout the community.
