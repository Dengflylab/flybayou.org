About Fly Bayou

From a small local fly meeting that began in Louisiana in 2020, Fly Bayou has grown into a vibrant regional network of Drosophila researchers spanning multiple states. Monthly virtual meetings promote scientific discussion, collaboration, trainee development, and networking.
