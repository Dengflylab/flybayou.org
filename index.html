About Fly Bayou

Founded in 2020 by Dr. Wu-Min Deng and SeYeon Chung, Fly Bayou began as a network connecting Drosophila researchers in New Orleans and Baton Rouge, Louisiana. It has since grown into a vibrant, multi-state community that fosters scientific exchange, collaboration, trainee development, and networking through monthly virtual meetings and biennial in-person meetings.
